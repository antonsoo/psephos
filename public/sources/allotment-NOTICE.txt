Ancient text: Constitution of the Athenians, conventionally attributed to
Aristotle, chapters 63-65, late fourth century BCE. Ancient text is public domain.
F. G. Kenyon, Aristotelis Atheniensium Respublica, Oxford, first published 1920.
The retained copy's imprint lists reprints through 1958. The editor's printed
supplies and critical apparatus remain visible. New selected transcriptions,
close English, word notes, diagrams and instructional model: Anthony Mercer,
2026, MIT (LICENSE.txt). These are a reading of this edition, not a new critical
edition or an archaeological reconstruction.
